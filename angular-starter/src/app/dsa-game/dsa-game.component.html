🎮 How to Play DSA Game Arena

🎯

Goal

Sort the colorful blocks from smallest to largest using the algorithm shown in the level. Follow the algorithm's logic to earn points!

📖

Learn the Algorithm

Each level teaches you a specific sorting algorithm. The Algorithm Guide shows you exactly what steps the algorithm follows. Pay attention to these steps!

✅

Correct vs Incorrect Moves

Correct moves follow the algorithm's logic and give you +50 points and a green flash! ✨

Incorrect moves don't follow the algorithm and give you -10 points with a red shake. 💭

👆

Controls

Desktop: Click and drag blocks to swap their positions.

Mobile: Tap and drag blocks with your finger.

🎚️

Difficulty Levels

Easy: Fewer blocks, perfect for learning

Medium: More blocks, better challenge

Hard: Most blocks, ultimate test!

🤖

AI Assistant

Stuck? The AI can give you hints or show you the full solution step-by-step. Use it to learn, then try again!

⭐

Scoring & Stars

Earn stars based on your performance:

  • Time: Faster completion = more points
  • Accuracy: More correct moves = higher score
  • Stars: Collect stars to unlock new levels!
🔓

Level Progression

Levels unlock as you earn stars. Each level requires a certain number of stars to unlock. Master easier algorithms first!

🎓

Learning Tip

Don't just sort blindly! Understand WHY each move is correct according to the algorithm. That's how you truly learn DSA!
